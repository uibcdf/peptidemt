   available target/peptide identities, molecular modifications, constructs,

   Do not silently pool Kd, Ki, IC50, cellular phenotype or structural contacts.
2. Assess whether a coherent independent reference panel is feasible before

   domain, observable and units, independent reference/evaluation split,

   unsupported cases and bounded resource/use plan. Implementation follows

## Ownership discipline

PeptideMT owns the biological question, scientific eligibility and evaluation/
selection policy, orchestration and interpretation. Molecular parsing, system
identity/chemistry, selections, transformations and geometry belong to MolSysMT.
Visualization belongs to MolSysViewer and docking to its qualified owner route.
Use the common MolSysSuite/MolI responsibility map before assigning recording,
attribution, generic workflow, modelling or evidence capabilities to other owners.

Reuse supported public operations and report their exact source/limitations.
Do not add a local FASTA/SMILES/structure parser, molecular feature engine,
binding scorer, generic persistence layer or generic execution framework to
PeptideMT as a shortcut. A report/proposal does not promise owner implementation
or waive scientific receiving. Respect external-publication permission rules.
