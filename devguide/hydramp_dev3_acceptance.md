## Meaning of the name and scope

Use **HydrAMP modernized with PyTorch** in descriptive prose. The exact provider
package is `hydramp-torch==0.0.0.dev3`; `dev3` identifies a development artifact
after earlier local candidates, not a new scientific model name, a public release
or a quality level. Preserve that version and hashes for reproducibility.

Acceptance means experimental AMP use of the reviewed resource and native route.
It is not restricted forever to the sixteen or 256 measured cases. Those cases
bound the evidence, rather than the intended number of future proposals. Future
runs still require their actual artifact/use review and shared-host call limits.
The present acceptance spends no new inference, PCA, descriptor or Macrel budget.

## Evidence and remaining limits

The [256-input campaign](hydramp_confidence_campaign.md) preserves all observed
generated token/string/batch return views. The selected [FP64 investigation](hydramp_amp_reference.md)
supports lower modern aggregate error, with genuine original pointwise advantages
retained. Those results support use and continued investigation, rather than
uniform numerical or biological superiority.

The [private H4 experiment](hydramp_h4_qualification.md) retains 32 proposals,
30 compatible Macrel assessments and reproducible selection. Nine assessed
proposals are AMP-positive, all also Hemo-positive; no joint AMP-positive/NonHemo
hit occurs. Both sequence pools match retained original HydrAMP outputs. The
separately executed public installed adapter returns sixteen proposals with exact
same-provider native results/trace/replay and captured parent attribution. These
remain distinct proofs; a single all-public live chain was not rerun.

The observed public-adapter case uses about 432 MiB sampled aggregate process-tree
RSS and 8.36 s, within the 4 GB task intent. This does not qualify other hosts,
dependency versions or general performance. The original failed qualifier startup
and corrected limit-inheritance guards remain preserved.

Original notices, attribution and existing local-research conditions remain in
force. Starter-versus-original-Drive byte identity remains unverified; the
acceptance establishes no new redistribution or commercial/output-use clearance.
No package/model release, training, analogue generation, feedback, target
conditioning, GPU or new family is authorized.
