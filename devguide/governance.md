## Local work and common owners

The mandatory root entry delegates the complete shared policy set to
[MOLSYSSUITE_GUIDE.md](../MOLSYSSUITE_GUIDE.md), including tools, contribution
routes, owning-issue feedback, domain ownership and release/resource rules.
Use its common policy index and the actual registered provider guides when a
boundary becomes applicable; do not keep another copied common-rule table here.

PeptideMT owns the peptide-specific design question, scientific requirements,
occurrence mappings, evaluation/selection policy, composition and interpretation
under its approved scientific work order. Review `design_methodology.md`, D35/D36
and M1–M8 before implementation. The common owner map routes molecular-system
operations to MolSysMT, visualization to MolSysViewer and topography to TopoMT;
actual missing capabilities are reported to those owners with consumer evidence.
Preserve the separate responsibilities of Praxis, Recorda, Sabueso, Nextia and
MolSys-AI. Do not create speculative runtime dependencies or new scientific scope.

The local source-only package's commands are in root `AGENTS.md`. The named
shared environment is the routine workspace; member recipes remain clean
CI/qualification fixtures. Read [the optional-engine review](optional_engine_review.md)
when the commissioned W2 boundary actually appears.

closure with Conda and installs an immutable owner-source revision before testing.
See the [consumer migration record](archive/delegate_fasta_to_molsysmt.md).

[W3 assessment](w3_assessment.md) records actual authorized CPU execution,

The investigator's governance clarification supersedes only the historical

## Scientific attribution before live W3 acceptance

The W2 scientific method/result boundary makes the suite's
[optional attribution policy](https://github.com/uibcdf/molsyssuite/blob/main/devguide/ackredit_client_policy.md)
applicable. The owning review remains uibcdf/peptidemt#6; this source/guide
receiving does not install Ackredit or claim client adoption. The subsequent
component-owned [portable boundary](w2_macrel.md) implements lazy public capture
and detached host bibliography, with real-provider engine-free controls; its
current evidence and actual installed-science obligations remain distinct.
Before declaring
live scientific acceptance, complete the applicable host bibliography, actual
method/software identity, detached saved provenance and optional-provider
receiving. Preserve explicit absence/failure, application-owned sessions and
credit only for the branch actually reached. A saved reader does not create new
scientific credit. Choose a supported capability/floor explicitly and retain
real provider, compatibility and boundary-test evidence for any claimed client
integration; mocks or a guide copy alone are insufficient.

No generic digestion or quantity dependency is added by this receiving. Retain
W1's reviewed ordinary type/count/policy errors and unitless score semantics;
reassess ArgDigest/PyUnitWizard when those boundaries change. Exact Macrel
assets/terms/fixtures, authorized live host limits, native parity/M1–M8 and
SMonitor's src-layout audit uibcdf/smonitor#54 retain their separate evidence
and owners. No new scientific or publication authority is granted here.
