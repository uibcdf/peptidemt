## Why

Functioning modern inference and finite sequence fidelity justify experimental
use, while scientific equivalence, efficacy and generality remain separate
questions. The retained H4 pool has no joint Macrel AMP-positive/NonHemo hit.
Its 32 proposals and 30 compatible assessments are procedural evidence, not a
general biological benchmark. Global native AMP ranking remains non-equivalent.
A useful independent modernization and possible upstream collaboration do not
depend on creating a distinct novel algorithm.

## What was refuted

- Modernizing the runtime alone does not establish a new learned model,
  other-family capability, biological superiority or universal equivalence.
- Pointwise numerical superiority is not uniform: the original is closer for
  58 of the selected 192 FP64 diagnostic rows. Preserve the causal explanation
  and genuine original advantages alongside modern aggregate improvement.
- A successful public generation case and private H4 experiment do not become
  a newly executed all-public live pipeline by combining their reports.
- An issue in our fork does not authorize contacting the original authors or
  publishing a third-party issue/PR. Their interest remains unknown.
