## How

## Why

Faithful generation/assessment/selection is a useful foundation, but current
PeptideMT does not yet implement general redesign, adaptive optimization or
joint target/function design. More provider modernization alone cannot establish
those capabilities or their utility. Distinguish incremental gaps from defects
in the deliberately narrower accepted contracts.

## What was refuted

- Optional engine loading does not make Macrel-specific result meanings generic.
- A ranking, successful process or stored target context does not satisfy the
  scientific requirements of a design problem.
- More predictors or higher optimized scores are not independent validation.
- A full new architecture, training programme or biological experiment is not a
  prerequisite for every useful computational increment.
- This review does not revoke D35/M1–M8 or require changing accepted selectors.

## Scope and exclusions

PeptideMT-wide capability and product review. Provider work remains under
HydrAMP #3–#8 and consumer #32; existing engineering issues retain their owners.
No new models, execution budget, training, feedback implementation, target route,
chemical transformation, package publication or author contact is commissioned.
Analogue implementation remains paused.
