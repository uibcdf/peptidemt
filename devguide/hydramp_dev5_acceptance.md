and repeats agree on that finite fixture. Retain these limits:

  There is no biological utility, improvement, efficacy, safety, calibration,
  general probability fidelity or other-family claim.
- Saved endpoints do not establish which implementation is more numerically
  accurate on these new inputs. Preserve previous independent numerical
  evidence and original pointwise advantages within their own scopes.
- Original-Drive byte identity, asset/redistribution/output-use questions and
  original attribution conditions retain their existing dispositions. Acceptance
